# Surface brief — GlowDL desktop app (all five pages)

Mode: Operate. Platform: desktop Qt (Windows/macOS). Code-led; no comp round.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GlowDL is an operator's asset manifest — a working ledger of media jobs, not a glossy storefront. It refuses the rounded-card SaaS dashboard the category ships.

OWN-WORLD: warm-black graphite ground (#0B0A09), paper-white ink (#F7F4ED), 1px hairline rules, **zero radius anywhere**, square cells and tags, tabular numerals in monospace for measurement, and colour spent only on state (amber in-progress, red failure, green done). No gradients, no shadows, no blur, no opacity animation.

STORY: the user pastes a link, sees what it is, picks a quality, and watches a job progress; confidence comes from alignment, density, and state that reads at a glance. Failure routes to a named cause and a repair.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 232px flat rail (brand mark, five square nav items, core version, self-check) against a warm-black ground; main column with 30px gutters: 26px page title and one muted line, a full-width bordered URL panel with input and a paper-white 解析 button, square site tags, then the parsed media panel — square 282×159 thumbnail, title, mono meta line, quality select and format link, three option checkboxes, and a paper-white 开始下载 button — all above the fold.

FORM: grounded candidate 7 of 7 — "reverse contact sheet / asset manifest": the visual language of a film contact sheet held in negative and an asset inventory ledger. It is the seventh (least obvious) direction in my grounded list, chosen by the roll to break the category rut. It lends type, palette, density, and one signature move: the **timecode line** — every job states its measured time and bytes in aligned tabular figures, with a square play/pause transport control.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
